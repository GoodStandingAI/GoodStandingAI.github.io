Annual Return Requirements for Alberta Corporations

📋 What is an Annual Return?

An annual return is the yearly filing that Alberta corporations (and some other entities) must submit to the Corporate Registry to keep their company information current and maintain good standing. It updates key details (registered office, directors, and shareholders) for the required reporting period.

Legal Requirements & Deadlines

⚖️ Legal Basis

  • • Alberta Business Corporations Act (RSA 2000, c B-9) s. 268: Requires every Alberta corporation to file an annual return each year
  • • Cooperatives: Follow the separate annual return procedure published by Corporate Registry; filing channels differ from ordinary corporations.
  • • Business Corporations Regulation (Alta Reg 118/2000): Sets filing deadlines and form requirements

📅 Filing Deadline

Due by the last day of the month following the corporation's anniversary of incorporation

Example: Corporation incorporated June 15 → Annual return due July 31 each year

⚠️ Important Note

Failure to file on time will put the corporation in non-compliance and can eventually lead to it being struck off or dissolved by Service Alberta.

Step-by-Step Filing Process

1

Gather Company Data

Collect current details as of the end of the anniversary month:

  • • Directors' names and addresses
  • • Officers' information
  • • Registered office address
  • • Up to five largest shareholders
  • • Shareholder addresses
  • • Voting share percentages
2

Complete the Form

Required Form: REG3062

Use the Alberta Corporate Registry Annual Return (Form REG3062)available from Service Alberta or registry agents.

3

Submit to Registry

Authorized Corporate Registry Service Provider

Submit to a Service Alberta registry agent with required fee and one piece of government-issued ID.

Cooperatives Follow a Separate Process

Ordinary Alberta corporate annual returns must go through an authorized service provider. Direct submission to Corporate Registry applies to cooperatives under theseparate cooperative instructions.

Pay Fees

Maintain Proof of Filing

Record Keeping Requirements

  • • Keep copy of filed Annual Return in corporate records
  • • Retain receipt or filing confirmation
  • • Note: Annual return is separate from income tax returns

Frequently Asked Questions

Who must file an annual return?

Every active Alberta-incorporated company must file one each year. This includes domestic corporations and extra-provincial corporations registered in Alberta (except those home-registered in BC, MB or SK).

What information is required?

The return must list up-to-date company information as of the anniversary date:

  • • Names and addresses of directors and officers
  • • Registered office address
  • • For share-capital corporations: up to five largest shareholders with share percentages
  • • Any changes since the last filing must be included

What happens if I miss the deadline?

Consequences of Late Filing:

  • • Corporation status shows "overdue"
  • • Continued non-filing can lead to strike-off
  • • Company name may be struck from register (dissolution)
  • • Once dissolved, corporation loses legal standing until revived

Are there fees to file?

Yes. Authorized service providers charge a government filing fee and a service fee. Confirm the current amounts with your provider before filing.

Note: There is no fee for filings by Alberta co-ops under the Co-op Act.

Understanding Corporate Compliance Requirements

Stay compliant and in good standing with comprehensive understanding of mandatory corporate filings, deadlines, and requirements across Canadian jurisdictions.

Registry agent service charges and any required name-search or professional fees are separate. Ask your provider for an itemised quote; fees and exemptions depend on the entity and transaction.

⚠️ Critical Compliance Alert

Failure to maintain corporate compliance can result in:

  • Corporate dissolution or striking from registry
  • Personal director liability
  • Inability to access banking or credit facilities
  • Penalties and late filing fees
  • Loss of legal standing in court proceedings

Annual Returns & Reports

Federal Corporations (CBCA)

Filing Deadline:Within 60 days after the incorporation, amalgamation or continuance anniversary (not in the year of that event)
Annual Fee:$12 (online government fee)
Late Penalty:Risk of administrative dissolution
Required Information:
  • Current directors and addresses
  • Registered office address
  • Anniversary-date information requested by the annual return
  • Individuals with significant control (ISC) information, filed with the annual return

Provincial Corporations

Requirements vary significantly by province. Always verify current requirements.

Ontario: Annual Return within 6 months of fiscal year-end; confirm the current OBR fee for the filing channel
British Columbia: Annual Report within 2 months of the incorporation anniversary; $43.39 filing fee
Alberta: Annual Return follows the incorporation anniversary and return notice; CAD $53.05 government fee plus provider charges
Quebec: Annual updating declaration and registration dues follow the REQ entity-specific filing period and tariff

Director & Officer Updates

Director Notices and Officer Records

  • • Alberta corporations: Director changes must be reported within 15 days.
  • • Out-of-province corporations registered in Alberta: Corporations from outside BC, Manitoba and Saskatchewan generally report director changes within 30 days; check the separate NWPTA process for those three provinces.
  • • Federal corporations: Report board changes within 15 days; director address changes are due within 15 days of the corporation being informed.
  • • Officer changes: Keep appointment and resignation records in the minute book. Check any registry requirements for the governing jurisdiction; Alberta does not require a director notice solely for an officer change.

Director Qualification Requirements

Federal Corporations (CBCA)

  • • Normally at least 25% resident Canadians; at least one if there are fewer than four directors. Sector-specific exceptions apply.
  • • At least 18 years old
  • • Not bankrupt
  • • Mentally competent

Required Information

  • • Full legal name
  • • Address accepted by the registry; a residential address is not always required
  • • Date of appointment/resignation
  • • Canadian residency status where the governing statute requires it

Registered Office Address Changes

⚠️ Important Timing Requirements

Report the change within the period that applies to the corporation. The federal and Alberta rules below run after the change; they are not a universal requirement to file before moving.

Federal (CBCA): Notify Corporations Canada within 15 days following the registered-office address change. Moving to a different province or territory also requires an amendment to the articles.
Alberta corporations: Give an authorized service provider the address update within 15 days. The registered office must remain a physical location in Alberta. Other jurisdictions and extra-provincial registrations have their own rules.

Annual Compliance Calendar

Around the Registry Anniversary

  • • Check the corporation's actual registry anniversary and filing window
  • • File federal returns within 60 days after the anniversary
  • • File BC reports within 2 months after the applicable anniversary
  • • Follow Alberta's annual return notice and entity-specific deadlines

Around the Financial Year and AGM

  • • Prepare financial statements and meeting materials
  • • Schedule the annual meeting or permitted written resolutions under the governing rules
  • • For Ontario corporations, track the annual return deadline of 6 months after fiscal year-end
  • • Keep corporate annual returns separate from income tax filings

Whenever Information Changes

  • • Report director and address changes within the applicable period
  • • Update the minute book and any required ownership or ISC registers
  • • Federal corporations must also file ISC changes within 15 days of a change to their ISC register
  • • Review licences, extra-provincial registrations and agent-for-service details
